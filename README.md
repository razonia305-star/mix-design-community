# Mix-Design AI Community

**More time to create. Less time to produce.**

Mix-Design AI is an AI-powered visual platform for architecture, interior design, real estate, construction and professional visualization.

This public repository is the community space around Mix-Design AI.

## What you can do here

- Propose new features
- Suggest improvements
- Report bugs
- Discuss AI workflows
- Share ideas for architecture and interior design tools
- Help improve 360° workflows
- Suggest improvements for SketchUp integration
- Discuss new AI models and integrations

## Main areas of Mix-Design AI

- Architecture
- Interior Design
- Virtual Staging
- Exterior Design
- 360° Visualization
- SketchUp Integration
- Materials and Textures
- AI Image Enhancement
- Razonia AI

## Community

We want Mix-Design AI to evolve with feedback from users, designers, architects, developers and AI enthusiasts.

Some parts of the project may become open for collaboration over time, while the core application remains private.

## Roadmap

Current areas of development include:

- Better 360° generation
- Improved camera consistency
- Better preservation of materials and textures
- Improved SketchUp workflows
- Razonia AI integration
- New AI models and providers
- Professional visualization tools
