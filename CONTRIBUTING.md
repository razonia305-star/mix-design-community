# Contributing to Mix-Design AI Community

Thanks for being part of the Mix-Design AI community.

This space is intended for people interested in architecture, interior design, visualization, AI, 3D workflows and related technologies to exchange ideas, experiments and experiences.

The goal is not simply to collect feature requests, but to learn from each other and explore better ways of working with AI in professional visual workflows.

## Ways to participate

You can contribute by:

- Sharing ideas and experiments
- Discussing workflows and approaches
- Reporting problems
- Suggesting improvements
- Sharing examples or results
- Comparing AI models and tools
- Helping others with questions
- Proposing new integrations
- Contributing code when a component is open for collaboration

## Discussions or Issues?

Use **Discussions** when you want to:

- Share an idea
- Start a conversation
- Compare approaches
- Ask a question
- Show something you have created

Use **Issues** when you want to:

- Report a specific problem
- Suggest a clearly defined feature or improvement

## A community of peers

You do not need to be a professional developer to participate.

Architects, designers, visualization artists, builders, developers, AI enthusiasts and people experimenting with new workflows are equally welcome.

Different backgrounds often lead to better ideas.

## Sharing knowledge

If you have found an interesting solution, workflow, model or approach, feel free to share it.

Likewise, if Mix-Design AI discovers something useful through experimentation, we want this community to be a place where those findings can also be discussed and shared.

## Respect

Keep conversations constructive and respectful.

Disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Security and privacy

Please do not publish:

- API keys
- Passwords
- Access tokens
- Private customer information
- Confidential project files
- Other sensitive information

## About the project

The core Mix-Design AI application is currently private.

Some tools, experiments and components may become available for open collaboration over time.
